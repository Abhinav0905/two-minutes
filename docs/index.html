What can I try?

Compare a fire confined to the pan with one that has already spread. Try leaving immediately. In the small-pan scenario, compare the lid-plus-heat sequence with selecting the water glass. The debrief records what you did and explains recovery.

How do I open the native Lens?
  1. Download the project and extract it.
  2. Open TwoMinutes.esproj in Lens Studio 5.23.2 or a compatible later version.
  3. Choose SPECS 27 and Interactive Preview. The recorded background is Evening Room. Right-drag to look toward the cooktop; click to simulate a pinch.
What has been checked?

The Lens passed 23 portable tests and seven native Preview cases. They cover both scenarios, containment, leaving, water recovery and reset. The browser companion imports the same decision engine. Real-glasses comfort, hand tracking and teaching effectiveness still need testing.

Where does the safety script come from?

The script draws on CPSC cooking-fire guidance, NFPA educational messages and USFA escape-plan guidance. A fire educator has not yet reviewed this prototype. Follow local fire-service guidance.
