# Safety script sources

Checked November 6, 2026. The prototype is an authored practice drill, not a sensor or a fire simulator.

- CPSC, Recipe for Safer Cooking: slide a lid over a grease or oil fire, turn off the heat, leave the lid until cool and never carry the pan outside. https://www.cpsc.gov/safety-education/safety-guides/home-fire/recipe-safer-cooking
- NFPA Educational Messages Desk Reference, cooking-fire guidance: when in doubt get out, close the door behind you and call the fire department from outside. Never pour water on a cooking-pan grease fire. https://www.nfpa.org/-/media/project/storefront/catalog/files/home-fire-safety/emac/educationalmessagesdeskreference.pdf?rev=967564ad45c84627bb02b5baef0dc12c
- USFA Home Fire Escape Plans: residents could have less than two minutes after an alarm sounds. The drill must not imply that two minutes is a safe allowance. https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/home-fire-escape-plans/
- USFA Cooking Fire Safety: cooking is the leading cause of home fires and home fire injuries. https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/cooking/

## Implementation boundaries

Leaving is accepted at every active stage. Closing the door is not a locked prerequisite for calling for help from outside. No points reward approaching a spreading fire. No scripted spread follows successful containment. No assertion says that crouching creates breathable air. No live route is certified safe. Scenario timers measure interaction only and do not predict a fire's development.
